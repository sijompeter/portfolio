03Fit

What I hold to, and where I’d be wasted.

I’d rather delete a step than decorate one.

Value 02

Evidence over taste

I bring the funnel and the recordings. No data, and I say so.

Value 03

Fewer surprises, later

I write the failure states, not just the happy path.
