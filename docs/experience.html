Jan 2023 — Mar 2026

Senior Product Designer

marketfeed · Fintech · Y Combinator S21 · Bangalore

Shaped the product through every pivot and was sole designer in each squad, across acquisition, activation and retention in turn, on a platform that reached ₹300 Cr+ AUM and 5,500+ users across iOS and Android.

  • Redesigned the Pledge & Trade onboarding experience, taking activation from 51% to 70%+ and cutting onboarding time by 11%.
  • Built a self-service fund allocation experience that replaced a manual operational workflow, 75% fewer support queries and 91% user adoption within three months.
  • Built the Keystone Design System from scratch, tokens, components, interaction patterns and Flutter specifications, adopted company-wide within six months.
  • Introduced AI-assisted workflows that cut feature scoping from roughly two hours to twenty minutes.
  • Used Mixpanel and Microsoft Clarity to find funnel drop-offs, validate hypotheses and direct what got built next.
  • Mentored the junior designers who joined behind me, some arriving from other fields, until they could carry their own squads’ work independently.
